Starting with a business outcome, working backwards into the process that produces it, and engineering everything required to move it.

what it is

The discipline of starting with a business outcome and working backwards into the process that produces it.

Searce practises AI Outcome Engineering as its core discipline across four engineering practices and eleven countries, engineering the AI, data, cloud, software and operating change required to move the number.

AI Outcome Engineering

Produces a measured change in a named business number, agreed before work begins and verified when it ends.

why ROI from AI remains unsolved

Most enterprises now have AI in production somewhere. Far fewer can attribute a change in their financial results to it.

Pilots are optimised for demonstration. Production is optimised for reliability, exception handling, integration and human trust. The distance between them is not a modelling problem.

what it actually involves

Six moves, in order, every time.

01 Starting from the number

Every Searce engagement begins with a business metric the client agrees should be dramatically better: a cycle time, a cost per transaction, a conversion rate, an error rate, a days-outstanding figure.

02 Working backwards into the process

Searce maps the process as it actually runs, using event logs, ticket flows, system telemetry and document trails rather than workshop diagrams. What people describe and what the logs show are usually different processes.

03 Building the metric ladder

The headline number is decomposed into the chain of steps that produce it, so it becomes clear which gaps are engineered and which are commercial.

04 Locking the baseline

The current value of each gap is computed from the client’s own systems, agreed in writing, and sealed with a date before any build begins.

05 Engineering the change

Whatever moves the number: process redesign, systems rebuilt, agents deployed, platform modernised, people retrained, guardrails built. Searce squads carry all four capabilities.

06 Verifying the reading

At the end, the same measurement is recomputed against the same sources and presented with the client rather than to them.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is AI Outcome Engineering different from AI consulting?

Consulting produces a recommendation and hands over. AI Outcome Engineering produces a measured change in a business number and stays accountable for it, including the build and the adoption.

What if the number does not move?

The verified reading reports what happened, including when the result disappoints. Searce presents the reading with the client either way, because a method that only reports successes is not a method.

Do you need a baseline before starting?

Yes. Searce does not begin build work without a locked baseline. Without one, no claim about the result can be made honestly.

How long before there is a result?

The entry engagement, Outcome Discovery, runs two to four weeks and produces the ladder and the baseline. A first verified movement typically follows a production build.
